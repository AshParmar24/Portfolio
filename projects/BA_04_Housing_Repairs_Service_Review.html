Housing Repairs Service Review

Client scenario. Soar Valley Homes is a housing association with about 6,200 homes. Its board report showed repairs completed within target slipping every quarter and complaints rising, while damp and mould reports had doubled since Awaab's Law came into force for social landlords on 27 October 2025. The Head of Property Services asked for an independent look at 18 months of repairs data: what has changed, where, and what should be done first.

My role. Business analyst. I defined the measures with the service, wrote the SQL, built the dashboard, and turned the findings into prioritised recommendations and the requirements that feed the business case in the next project.

What I found

FindingEvidenceSo what
1. The fall is one contractor, not the whole serviceKestrel, which covers the North and East areas, went from 84 to 63 percent within target from January 2026. Its first-time fix rate fell from 84 to 72 percent and average days to complete rose from 12.5 to 17.3. The in-house team and the gas contractor held at about 82 percent.About 823 extra return visits in ten months. This is a contract management issue, and a service-wide restructure would be the wrong response.
2. Phone bookings miss more than twice as oftenPhone-booked jobs average 0.24 no-access visits each; portal bookings, which send a text reminder, average 0.09. Only 24 percent of jobs are booked through the portal.1,675 wasted visits a year would not happen at the portal rate. Each one also adds a week or more to the job.
3. Damp and mould demand has doubled and is hard to fix first timeReports rose from 79 to 157 a month. First-time fix is 49 percent and 27 percent are reported again within 90 days. The share visited within 14 days has fallen from 76 to 66 percent.Significant damp and mould hazards must be investigated within 10 working days. The current process cannot evidence that, and performance is moving the wrong way.
4. Missing the target is what tenants noticeSatisfaction averages 4.34 out of 5 when a job is on time and fixed first time, and 2.58 when it is late and needs a return visit.Speed and first-time fix are the two levers for satisfaction.
5. Overdue work in progress is buildingJobs open and already past target rose from 68 at the end of October 2025 to 124 a year later.Without action the backlog will grow through winter, when demand peaks.

Requirements that came out of the analysis

  • Record how every appointment was booked and whether a reminder was sent, so no-access can be tracked by channel.
  • Record first visit date and outcome separately from completion, so investigation timescales can be evidenced.
  • Give contractors and the in-house team one diary, so a missed appointment is rebooked the same day.
  • Report within target, first-time fix and repeat reports by contractor and trade every week.

Limits

Fourteen calendar days is used as a simple stand-in for the 10 working day investigation duty. Jobs too recent to be judged against their target are excluded from the within-target measure. The data shows that Kestrel's performance changed in January 2026 but not why; that needs conversations with the contractor.

SQL behind the review

Ten named queries in SQLite. The work-in-progress query uses a recursive CTE to build month ends and count jobs open on each date.

sla_monthly_by_priority.sql
-- Share of jobs completed within target, by month reported and priority.
SELECT strftime('%Y-%m', reported_date) AS month,
       priority,
       COUNT(*) AS jobs,
       SUM(CASE WHEN completed_date IS NOT NULL
                 AND julianday(completed_date) - julianday(reported_date) <= target_days THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) AS within_target,
       ROUND(100.0 * SUM(CASE WHEN completed_date IS NOT NULL
                 AND julianday(completed_date) - julianday(reported_date) <= target_days THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) / COUNT(*), 1) AS within_target_pct
FROM fact_repair_jobs
WHERE julianday('2026-10-31') - julianday(reported_date) >= target_days   -- only jobs old enough to be judged
GROUP BY 1, 2
ORDER BY 1, 2;
sla_monthly_by_contractor.sql
-- The same measure by contractor, to see whether the fall is general or concentrated.
SELECT strftime('%Y-%m', reported_date) AS month,
       contractor,
       COUNT(*) AS jobs,
       SUM(CASE WHEN completed_date IS NOT NULL
                 AND julianday(completed_date) - julianday(reported_date) <= target_days THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) AS within_target,
       ROUND(100.0 * SUM(CASE WHEN completed_date IS NOT NULL
                 AND julianday(completed_date) - julianday(reported_date) <= target_days THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) / COUNT(*), 1) AS within_target_pct
FROM fact_repair_jobs
WHERE julianday('2026-10-31') - julianday(reported_date) >= target_days
GROUP BY 1, 2
ORDER BY 1, 2;
contractor_scorecard.sql
-- Contractor scorecard, first eight months against the latest ten (January 2026 onwards).
SELECT contractor,
       CASE WHEN reported_date < '2026-01-01' THEN '1. May to Dec 2025' ELSE '2. Jan to Oct 2026' END AS period,
       COUNT(*) AS jobs,
       ROUND(100.0 * SUM(CASE WHEN completed_date IS NOT NULL
                 AND julianday(completed_date) - julianday(reported_date) <= target_days THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) / COUNT(*), 1) AS within_target_pct,
       ROUND(100.0 * AVG(first_time_fix), 1)                                        AS first_time_fix_pct,
       ROUND(AVG(julianday(completed_date) - julianday(reported_date)), 1)          AS avg_days_to_complete,
       ROUND(AVG(satisfaction_score), 2)                                            AS avg_satisfaction,
       ROUND(AVG(job_cost), 2)                                                      AS avg_job_cost
FROM fact_repair_jobs
WHERE julianday('2026-10-31') - julianday(reported_date) >= target_days
GROUP BY 1, 2
ORDER BY 1, 2;
no_access_by_channel.sql
-- No-access visits by how the appointment was booked. Portal bookings send a text reminder.
SELECT booking_channel,
       COUNT(*)                                                         AS jobs,
       SUM(no_access_visits)                                            AS no_access_visits,
       ROUND(100.0 * SUM(CASE WHEN no_access_visits > 0 THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) / COUNT(*), 1) AS jobs_with_no_access_pct,
       ROUND(1.0 * SUM(no_access_visits) / COUNT(*), 3)                 AS no_access_visits_per_job
FROM fact_repair_jobs
WHERE priority <> 'Emergency'
GROUP BY booking_channel
ORDER BY jobs DESC;
first_time_fix_by_trade.sql
-- First-time fix, repeat reports and cost by trade (completed jobs).
SELECT trade,
       COUNT(*)                                        AS completed_jobs,
       ROUND(100.0 * AVG(first_time_fix), 1)           AS first_time_fix_pct,
       ROUND(100.0 * AVG(repeat_within_90_days), 1)    AS repeat_within_90_days_pct,
       ROUND(AVG(job_cost), 2)                         AS avg_job_cost,
       ROUND(SUM(job_cost))                            AS total_cost
FROM fact_repair_jobs
WHERE status = 'Completed'
GROUP BY trade
ORDER BY first_time_fix_pct;
damp_mould_monthly.sql
-- Damp and mould: reports per month and the share visited within 14 calendar days.
-- 14 calendar days is used as a simple stand-in for the 10 working day investigation duty under Awaab's Law.
SELECT strftime('%Y-%m', reported_date) AS month,
       COUNT(*) AS reports,
       ROUND(100.0 * SUM(CASE WHEN first_visit_date IS NOT NULL
                 AND julianday(first_visit_date) - julianday(reported_date) <= 14 THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) / COUNT(*), 1) AS visited_within_14_days_pct
FROM fact_repair_jobs
WHERE trade = 'Damp & Mould'
  AND julianday('2026-10-31') - julianday(reported_date) >= 14
GROUP BY 1
ORDER BY 1;
open_jobs_month_end.sql
-- Work in progress: jobs open at each month end (reported on or before, not completed by, that date).
WITH RECURSIVE month_ends(d) AS (
    SELECT date('2025-05-31')
    UNION ALL
    SELECT date(d, 'start of month', '+2 months', '-1 day') FROM month_ends WHERE d < '2026-10-31'
)
SELECT m.d AS month_end,
       COUNT(*) AS open_jobs,
       SUM(CASE WHEN julianday(m.d) - julianday(j.reported_date) > j.target_days THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) AS open_past_target
FROM month_ends m
JOIN fact_repair_jobs j
  ON j.reported_date <= m.d
 AND (j.completed_date IS NULL OR j.completed_date > m.d)
GROUP BY m.d
ORDER BY m.d;
satisfaction_by_outcome.sql
-- Tenant satisfaction (1 to 5) by whether the job met target and was fixed first time.
SELECT CASE WHEN julianday(completed_date) - julianday(reported_date) <= target_days THEN 'Within target' ELSE 'Missed target' END AS target_outcome,
       CASE WHEN first_time_fix = 1 THEN 'Fixed first time' ELSE 'Needed a return visit' END AS fix_outcome,
       COUNT(satisfaction_score)          AS responses,
       ROUND(AVG(satisfaction_score), 2)  AS avg_satisfaction
FROM fact_repair_jobs
WHERE satisfaction_score IS NOT NULL
GROUP BY 1, 2
ORDER BY avg_satisfaction DESC;
avoidable_cost_last_12_months.sql
-- Avoidable activity in the last 12 months (November 2025 to October 2026), used in the business case.
SELECT COUNT(*)                                                                   AS jobs_reported,
       SUM(no_access_visits)                                                      AS no_access_visits,
       SUM(CASE WHEN booking_channel = 'Phone' THEN 1 ELSE 0 END)                 AS phone_booked_jobs,
       SUM(CASE WHEN status = 'Completed' THEN completed_visits - 1 ELSE 0 END)   AS return_visits,
       SUM(CASE WHEN repeat_within_90_days = 1 THEN 1 ELSE 0 END)                 AS repeat_reports,
       ROUND(SUM(job_cost))                                                       AS total_job_cost,
       ROUND(AVG(job_cost / completed_visits), 2)                                 AS avg_cost_per_visit
FROM fact_repair_jobs
WHERE reported_date >= '2025-11-01';
sla_cube.sql
-- Monthly jobs and jobs within target by contractor, priority and area. This feeds the dashboard filters.
SELECT strftime('%Y-%m', reported_date) AS month, contractor, priority, area,
       COUNT(*) AS jobs,
       SUM(CASE WHEN completed_date IS NOT NULL
                 AND julianday(completed_date) - julianday(reported_date) <= target_days THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) AS within_target,
       SUM(COALESCE(first_time_fix, 0)) AS fixed_first_time,
       SUM(CASE WHEN status = 'Completed' THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) AS completed_jobs,
       SUM(no_access_visits) AS no_access_visits
FROM fact_repair_jobs
WHERE julianday('2026-10-31') - julianday(reported_date) >= target_days
GROUP BY 1, 2, 3, 4;
