# Business Case: Repairs Scheduling and Tenant Self-Service

Options appraisal for Soar Valley Homes, a fictional housing association. Baseline volumes come from the repairs service review (BA 04); cost assumptions are illustrative.

## What is in this folder
| File | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `Repairs_Scheduling_Business_Case_Model.xlsx` | Nine-sheet model with live formulas: assumptions, baseline, options, cash flows, sensitivity, scoring, risks, benefits plan |

## Result
| Option | NPV (5 years) | Benefit cost ratio | Payback |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0. Do nothing | £0 | n/a | n/a |
| 1. Process fixes only | £225,921 | 4.74 | Year 1 |
| 2. Off-the-shelf system | £150,412 | 1.33 | Year 4 |
| 3. Bespoke build | (£155,243) | 0.76 | Beyond year 5 |

## Recommendation
Do the process fixes now. Take the off-the-shelf system to procurement only as a compliance and service investment: it scores highest on non-financial criteria (4.2 out of 5) but is £75,509 worse than Option 1 over five years.

## How to use the model
Change the blue cells on the Assumptions and Options sheets. Everything else recalculates.
